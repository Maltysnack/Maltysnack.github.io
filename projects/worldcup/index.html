How it works

Model. Elo ratings from every international since 1872, turned into win/draw/loss chances by a goals model.

Bets. When our chance times Sportsbet's price promises 3% profit or more, the bot puts $10 of fake money on it, once per match, up to 48 hours before kickoff. No prices above 6.0: on longshots a small disagreement looks like a huge edge.

Beat the close. Did we get a better price than the final one before kickoff? The fairest test of skill. Bankroll is mostly luck over a few dozen bets.

Ledger. Each bet is hashed and chained to the last, with the raw odds saved as evidence. Anyone can re-verify it.

Until it goes live. The bankroll sits at $1,000. No backtest numbers are shown, because we have no real past Sportsbet prices to test against.
